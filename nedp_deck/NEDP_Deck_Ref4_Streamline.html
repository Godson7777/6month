Job Description Execution · Supply Chain (1/2)

Before Any Price Goes Out, I Clarify the Demand and Read the Drawing

MAIN PROCESS · STEPS 1–3
1Demand Check
  • See demand rise in PCR
  • Clarify with BC: reason to buy (expansion / new business / replacement)
  • Project location; fast or low-cost priority
  • Expected price and estimated PO date
EVIDENCE
2Preliminary Drawing Review
  • Check unit function and how it works
  • Flag critical main components on price and lead time
  • Consult Application Engineering
EVIDENCE
3Request Quote to UTE
  • Attach prelim, expected price & lead time, end customer, expected delivery
  • Check standard GP (13.6% — pre-negotiation price)
  • Track SLA, escalate if late; log result in PCR
EVIDENCE
